Privacy Policy for Python Quest

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Floating Rocks ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Python Quest mobile application, also referred to as 30 Days Python.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Python Quest application.

1. Information We Collect

Python Quest does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to use the core learning features.

Some app preferences and course progress may be stored locally on your device so that the application can remember your progress and settings.

2. Advertising

Python Quest uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Advertisements may be displayed on selected non-learning sections of the application, such as the Home, Latest Python News, and Job Opportunities sections.

Advertisements are not intentionally displayed within the core course lessons, quizzes, coding playground, glossary, interview preparation, or certificate experience.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, and information related to advertising interactions in accordance with their applicable policies and user choices.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit Google's Privacy Policy .

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

Python Quest uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where applicable to manage privacy and consent choices for advertising.

Where applicable, users may be presented with a consent or privacy choices message based on their location and applicable regulations.

4. Course Progress and App Preferences

Python Quest may store information locally on your device, including course progress, quiz progress, language preferences, and other application settings.

This information is used to provide the application's features and is not intended to identify you personally.

5. Python Playground

Python Quest includes a Python coding playground that allows users to practice Python code.

Code entered into the playground is processed as part of the application's coding experience. Users should avoid entering passwords, financial information, personal identification information, or other sensitive information into the playground.

6. News and Job Opportunities

Python Quest may provide links to external websites for Python news and job opportunities.

When you open an external website, that website's own privacy policy and terms apply. Floating Rocks does not control the privacy practices of external websites.

7. Certificate

Python Quest may provide a certificate of completion after the applicable course and interview preparation requirements are completed.

Certificate generation is part of the application experience and does not require you to create an online account.

8. Children's Privacy

Python Quest is an educational application. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Third-Party Services

Python Quest may use third-party services necessary to provide application functionality, advertising, content, or external links.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
